## Summary

Cookie Pulse is a small, focused cApp that lets a user connect a Nightly-compatible wallet, read the latest Cookie Chain block, publish a short memo pulse, and wait for confirmation. The app never requests or stores private keys; every transaction is explicitly signed by the user.

## Evidence to capture before submission

- Screenshot of the connected wallet state.
- Screenshot of a confirmed transaction signature.
- CookieScan explorer link for the signature, if the explorer supports the network.
- Screenshot of the latest block read from the Cookie Chain RPC.

## Remaining verification

Confirm the public RPC and memo instruction are accepted by Cookie Chain, verify Nightly injection on the deployed origin, and add the required X thread and Cookie Chain Telegram post only after the application is tested end to end.
